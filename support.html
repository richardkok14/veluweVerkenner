Frequently asked

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. The map is bundled with the app and works fully offline — no connection or download is needed while you're out.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Only to show your position on the map and to record a track while you're actively recording. Location is used on your device only and is never sent anywhere. You can grant or revoke it any time in your device's Settings.

Where are my recorded tracks stored?

On your device only. Recorded tracks and imported GPX files never leave your phone. Removing the app removes this data.
