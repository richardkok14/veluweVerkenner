Privacy Policy — Veluwe Verkenner

Last updated: 11 June 2026

Veluwe Verkenner (“the app”) is an offline map for hiking and cycling in the Veluwe. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: the app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything the app does happens on your device.

Information we collect

None. Veluwe Verkenner has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. The app makes no network requests to us or to any third party while you use it — the maps are bundled with the app and work fully offline.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your device's location to:

  • show your current position on the map, and
  • record a track while you are actively recording.

This location data is used only on your device, while the app is in the foreground. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we never see it. The app does not use background location. You can grant or revoke location access at any time in your device's Settings.

Data stored on your device

The following stay on your device only and are never uploaded:

  • Recorded tracks and any GPX files you import.
  • App settings (such as map blend, track colour, and display preferences).

Removing the app removes this data from your device.

Sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose any data, because the app does not collect any.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data, it collects no data from children.

Map data attribution

The app displays open geodata that is licensed by its providers (elevation data © AHN; background map © Kadaster / PDOK; place data © OpenStreetMap contributors). This is licensing attribution, not data collection — no personal data is involved.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. Any data the app creates lives on your device and is under your control.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new “Last updated” date.
